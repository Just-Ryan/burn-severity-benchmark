## 1. The fee situation — free, if you choose correctly

CMPB is **hybrid**: it "offers authors two choices to publish their research" — open access or subscription. (The word "hybrid" is not used; the two-route structure is.)

| | Verbatim | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| Gold OA APC | "Article Publishing Charge (APC): **USD 3,180** (excluding taxes)" | 2026-07-25 |
| **Subscription route** | **"No open access publication fee."** / "No publication fee charged to authors, and published articles are immediately available to subscribers" | 2026-07-25 |
| Review is unaffected | "Your publication choice will have no effect on the peer review process" | 2026-07-25 |

**Choose the subscription route. You pay USD 0.** Green OA embargo for self-archiving is **12 months**.

Note: "During submission you will be presented with a personalized OA Article Publishing Charge" — USD 3,180 is the list price, and a personalised (possibly lower) figure may be shown. **It is still a choice you decline.**

### ⚠️ Do NOT submit to "CMPB Update"

**Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine Update** (eISSN 2666-9900) is a **separate, fully gold-OA journal** with **no free subscription route**:

> "As an open access journal with no subscription charges, a fee" is payable.

APCs: **USD 2,390** (full-length) / **USD 1,580** (short). DOAJ confirms `has_apc: true`. It participates in Elsevier's Geographical Pricing for Open Access, which reduces the price by national income — but a reduced fee is still a fee, and your budget is zero.

CMPB's own scope calls Update "the companion title." **They are not interchangeable.** If a CMPB editor offers to transfer your rejected paper to Update, that transfer costs money. Decline it.

## 4. Scope — the honest assessment

> "To encourage the development of formal computing methods, and their application in biomedical research" and medical practice.
> "to report new computer methodologies applied in biomedical areas"
> "the eventual distribution of demonstrable software to avoid duplication of effort"
> "to provide a forum for discussion and improvement of existing software"
> — Aims and scope, verified 2026-07-25

**Software papers are explicitly welcomed.** That is genuinely favourable — your deployed system plus released benchmark code fits the "demonstrable software" clause.

**Two honest caveats:**

- **Evaluation/benchmark papers: UNVERIFIED.** CMPB neither welcomes nor excludes them in writing.
- **The "no methodological novelty" rejection filter: UNVERIFIED — no such published statement exists.** The full Aims and Scope page, the complete Guide for Authors (62,410 characters) and the announcements block were all searched on 2026-07-25 and contain **no** statement rejecting papers that merely apply existing ML. If such a filter operates at CMPB it is editorial practice, not documented policy. **Do not repeat a secondary-source claim to the contrary.** That said, the scope's repeated emphasis on "formal computing methods" and "new computer methodologies" is the closest documented signal, and it does not favour a pure evaluation paper.

**Practical read:** CMPB is a reasonable but not obvious fit. Lead with the released software and the methodological *procedure* (source-grouped splitting, perceptual-hash contamination screening) rather than with the null result, and you are inside the stated scope. Lead with "we compared architectures" and you are not.

## 6. The generative-AI declaration

Same Elsevier policy as *Burns*. Verified 2026-07-25:

- **Section title, verbatim:** "Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process."
- **Placement:** "placed in a new section before the references list", added "at the end of the manuscript when the paper is first submitted."
- **Template:** "During the preparation of this work the author(s) used [NAME OF TOOL / SERVICE] in order to [REASON]. After using this tool/service, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the published article."
- **Exemption:** "does not apply to the use of basic tools, such as tools used to check grammar, spelling and references."
- **AI as research method is out of scope of this policy:** "This policy does not prevent the use of AI tools in formal research design or research methods." Your segmentation and classification models are method and do **not** belong in this declaration.
- AI cannot be an author. AI-generated figures must be disclosed in each caption **and** in the general statement.

Your existing declaration already matches the template. Move it; do not shorten it.
